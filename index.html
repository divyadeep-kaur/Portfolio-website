About me

I'm Divyadeep, a second-year B.Tech student in Computer Science and AI at DAV University, Jalandhar, in Punjab.

I like turning an idea into something people can open and use. So far that has meant FocusForge AI, StockSense AI and PawGuard AI, which maps stray dogs, plus three hackathons, one of them a first runner-up finish.

Away from the screen I draw, and I make things by hand from cardboard and waste. I won a district-level drawing competition, and I have been Head Girl and a class representative, so leading a team and speaking in front of people feel natural to me. I also run a YouTube channel.
